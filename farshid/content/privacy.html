Privacy

pirahansiah.com is a wholly static site. There are no analytics scripts, no cookies, no trackers, and no server-side logging of personal data.

  • What we collect: Nothing. Pages are plain static files served over HTTPS.
  • External links: Some pages link to third-party sites (publishers, GitHub, YouTube). Their privacy policies govern once you leave this site.
  • Media embeds: Audio and images hosted by external services (e.g. Spotify) may set their own cookies.
