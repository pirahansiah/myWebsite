Solana Arcade — Snake & Tetris

◎ Play Snake and Tetris — connect Phantom, play, then sign your best score. Signing is a message signature only: it is free, sends no transaction and spends no SOL.

  • Phantom wallet connection to prove the address that earned a score; no account and no install.
