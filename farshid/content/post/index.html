Mentoring, Consulting & Coaching

I mentor engineers and consult for teams building computer vision and edge AI systems that have to survive production — from camera calibration and image processing to CUDA kernels, model optimization and on-device LLMs.

12+ years in the field · 21 publications · 3 patents.

What I do

  • One-on-one mentoring & tutoring — your pace, your code, your problem, from CV fundamentals to edge deployment
  • Group workshops — one topic worked through together, with hands-on exercises
  • Project-based coaching — architecture, milestones and debugging while you build
  • Code review — a read of your implementation, with concrete suggestions
  • Career guidance — mentorship for computer vision careers and research
  • Consulting & engineering — when the system has to ship: real-time vision, GPU acceleration, model optimization, on-device LLMs

The resources

  • 10 Years of CV Debugging Lessons — lessons learned from a decade of debugging computer vision systems in production.
  • Tips and tricks to run a local model for Hermes Agent on your laptop — budgeting a 32k context between instructions, references and the answer, and a yes/no filter that picks the chunks before the main model sees them.
  • CV Coaching & Teaching Roadmap — the curriculum I teach, from image formation to multi-camera systems.
  • OpenCV 5 Cheat Sheet (PDF) — the functions that come up in every session, on one page.
  • Computer Vision Coaching Roadmap (PDF) — the same learning path as a printable sheet.
