About Me

I build and advise computer-vision and AI systems, connecting research with practical engineering for edge and cloud. I also mentor engineers who want to deepen their skills in computer vision, GPU optimization, and on-device AI.

Areas of work

  • Computer Vision & AI — image processing, deep learning, real-time systems
  • Edge AI — Jetson, Coral TPU, Intel NCS2, Raspberry Pi, FPGA (Kria KV260)
  • On-Device LLMs — local inference, model quantization (INT8/FP16), TensorRT, OpenVINO
  • GPU Optimization — CUDA, RTX 1080→5090, Apple Silicon
  • Full-Stack LLM — training, fine-tuning, deployment, API integration

How I help

  • Consulting & engineering — design and build computer-vision and AI systems for production.
  • Coaching & tutoring — a practical learning plan, shaped around your goals and experience.
