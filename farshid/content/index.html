Hardware & optimization expertise

These are the stacks I work in every week: NVIDIA Jetson (AGX Orin, Orin Nano, Xavier), Google Coral TPU, Hailo-8 and Kria KV260 FPGA; TensorRT, OpenVINO and ONNX Runtime; CUDA and cuDNN; INT8/FP16 quantization and calibration; C++17/20 with OpenCV and G-API; real-time multi-camera capture, synchronization and on-device LLMs.

  • CV, DL & ML optimization guide — the method I follow, technique by technique.
  • CUDA development environment and PyCUDA: C kernels from Python.
  • 3D vision & real-time multi-camera systems · cabling for multi-camera capture and split inference.

Problems I am usually called in for

  • A detector running at 4 FPS on the Jetson that was already bought — quantization, TensorRT engines and GPU-side pre-processing.
  • A prototype that works on the workstation and falls apart on the device — measuring the real memory and thermal budget.
  • Cameras that do not line up, or a system that loses frames under load — optical flow, calibration and multi-camera synchronization.
  • A model that has to run on-device with no cloud and no GPU — image generation on 8 GB, firmware on a €3 board.
